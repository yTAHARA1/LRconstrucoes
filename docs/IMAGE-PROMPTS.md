# Imagens institucionais geradas

Modo usado: ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens do Codex.

## Capa — `img/servicos/hero-manutencao-predial.webp`

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: wide landing-page hero image for a Brazilian building maintenance company website
Primary request: create a premium, realistic editorial photograph of trained building-maintenance professionals performing exterior facade painting and inspection using rope-access equipment on a contemporary mid-rise building
Scene/backdrop: clean urban architecture in Brazil, modern facade, subtle distant city atmosphere
Subject: two adult professionals in complete safety gear, helmets, harnesses and secured ropes, carefully working on the facade; safe professional posture
Style/medium: high-end commercial architectural photography, natural materials and believable details
Composition/framing: very wide cinematic landscape; workers and building concentrated on the right half, generous darker facade/sky negative space on the left for website headline; strong diagonal architectural lines; no close identifiable faces
Lighting/mood: warm early-morning sunlight, confident, reliable, energetic, professional
Color palette: deep navy shadows, warm construction orange accents, neutral concrete and white facade
Constraints: realistic rope-access rigging and PPE; no text; no logos; no watermarks; no company branding; no claims or badges; suitable as a website background with high readability overlay
Avoid: artificial CGI appearance, dramatic danger, unsafe behavior, clutter, warped hands, impossible ropes, low resolution
```
